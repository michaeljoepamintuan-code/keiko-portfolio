8modules, one platform
  • Live workforce dashboard. On-time, late, absent, on-leave, overbreak and overpunch counts in Philippine time, plus pending overtime and the next payroll date.
  • Biometric attendance import. Uploads monthly punch records from ZKBioTime / Yunatt devices, plus WFH attendance and attendance reports.
  • Team scheduling. Immediate heads plot schedules with drag-and-drop, then share an approved-schedule link and keep a full change history.
  • E-signed requests & approvals. Schedule changes, leave, overtime and offsets. The employee signs, then the Immediate Head approves.
  • Org structure. A drag-and-drop org chart built from reporting lines, across companies, branches, departments and job titles.
  • Payroll & BIR compliance. Allowances and charges, plus BIR 1601-C and 0619-E withholding tax figures computed from payroll records.
  • Employee self-service. Every employee sees their own punches, lates, absences and overtime bank per pay period.
  • HRIS design
  • Process automation
  • Approval workflows
  • DOLE & BIR compliance
  • No-code / AI app building
